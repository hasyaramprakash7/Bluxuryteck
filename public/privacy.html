Privacy Policy

B Luxury Private Limited (BLuxury Tech) · Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy explains how B Luxury Private Limited, operating as BLuxury Tech ("we", "us", "our"), collects and uses personal information when you visit our website, contact us, or interact with a WhatsApp messaging service that we build and operate, including our real-estate enquiry assistant used by property developers.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us through our website

  • The details you enter in our project brief form, such as your name, company, work email, timeline, and project description. When you submit the form, it opens WhatsApp with your brief pre-filled; the information reaches us only when you send that WhatsApp message.
  • Any messages you send us on WhatsApp, email, or Instagram.

When you interact with a WhatsApp service we operate for a business

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name you gave the business or that appears on your WhatsApp profile.
  • Your replies and selections, such as the property configuration you are interested in, your budget range, and your preferred site-visit date and time.
  • Message status information provided by WhatsApp, such as whether a message was delivered or read.

Automatically, when you browse our website

  • Standard technical data that our hosting provider records to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested.
  • Your light/dark theme choice, stored only in your own browser. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

We do not ask for payment details, government ID numbers, or passwords through these services.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, and deliver the services you request from us.
  • On behalf of the businesses we serve: to send you information about the property project you enquired about (including brochures and location details), understand your requirements, schedule and confirm site visits, and notify that business's sales team.
  • To respect your opt-out and stop sending you messages when you ask.
  • To keep our website and services secure and to fix technical problems.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Who we share it with

  • The business you enquired with (for example, the real-estate developer whose project you are interested in), so their team can follow up with you.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp), which delivers WhatsApp messages under the WhatsApp Privacy Policy.
  • Service providers who host our website, servers, databases, and files (such as cloud hosting and storage providers) and process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, where we are required to do so by law.

4. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and messaging information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and no longer than 24 months after your last interaction, unless the law requires a longer period. You can ask us to delete it sooner (see section 6).

5. Stopping WhatsApp messages

Reply STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, or NOT INTERESTED to any message from a WhatsApp service we operate. We will mark your number as opted out and stop sending you updates about that project. You can also block the number in WhatsApp at any time.

6. Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, in line with applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

To request deletion, email privacy@bluxury.shop with the subject "Data deletion request" and the phone number or email address you used with us. We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

7. Security

We protect your information with access controls, encrypted connections (HTTPS), and restricted administrator access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the relevant authorities of a breach where the law requires.

8. Children

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed.
